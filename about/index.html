About

I am a Senior Researcher at Ericsson Ireland working on autonomous network management, multi-agent systems, information constraints and related questions in economics and artificial intelligence.

My research includes theoretical work on task-relevant information and coordination, applied work on O-RAN and autonomous systems, and cross-disciplinary work connecting information, institutions, cognition and adaptive learning.
